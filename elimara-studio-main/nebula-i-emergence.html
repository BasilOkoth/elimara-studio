THE WORK
Emergence explores the moment order begins to surface from repetition.

Emergence explores the moment order begins to surface from repetition. Curved geometric fields accumulate, echo and shift until the system feels almost alive — suspended between code, rhythm and perception.

The physical collector edition is conceived at 700 × 1000 mm to preserve both the overall geometry and the fine internal detail. The work is produced only when acquired, then signed and numbered within the declared edition of 25.

Each collector receives the physical artwork together with a matching Certificate of Authenticity and rear artwork label identifying the work, year, edition, medium, dimensions and certificate reference.

Scale changes the experience.

The 70 × 100 cm format is designed to read both architecturally across a room and intricately at close range.
