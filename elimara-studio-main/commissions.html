THE PROCESS

From room to finished work.

A clear commissioning process protects both the client and the artistic direction while keeping decisions manageable.

01

Space

Send a wall photo, dimensions, location and intended atmosphere.

02

Direction

The studio develops initial visual directions around the space and brief.

03

Approval

One direction is refined within the agreed revision allowance.

04

Production

The approved work is produced to the agreed material, scale and finish.

05

Delivery

Documentation, delivery and installation options are coordinated as quoted.
